| Item | BAH | LMI |

| Vesting | Immediate | Immediate |
| Service requirement | None | **1 year** |
| Lock-in / retention mechanic | **Must be active 12/31 to receive that year's match.** Mid-year exit forfeits ~$8,700/yr | None after year 1 — full portability |

**Plain reading:**
- **BAH penalizes mid-year exit every year.** At $145K, a mid-year separation forfeits ~$8,700. This is a recurring retention tax, not a one-time cost.
- **LMI penalizes exit before year 1.** Stay past month 12 and every dollar is portable, vested, yours.

For someone who plans to stay 3+ years, LMI's structure is friendlier; for a one-year-and-out plan, BAH is friendlier. Thomas's compass is the former.
