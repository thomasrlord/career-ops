## The 401(k) Story (Important — Probe at Offer Stage)

LMI's 401(k) structure:

| Employee contributes | LMI matches | Plus LMI auto-contributes |
|---|---|---|
| 1% | 1% | 3% of base |
| 2% | 2% | 3% of base |
| **3%+** | **3% (cap)** | **3% of base** |

**Max LMI contribution: 6% of base salary, 100% vested immediately.**

The 3% non-elective auto-contribution is real value — it's not a match, it's paid regardless of whether Thomas contributes. At $145K base that's $4,350/yr non-elective + up to $4,350/yr matched = $8,700/yr LMI 401(k) value.

**The catch: "after completion of one year of service."**
- Question to ask: does the 1-year wait apply to BOTH the match AND the non-elective? Or just one?
- Question to ask: can the 1-year service requirement be waived for senior hires?
- Compare to BAH's 401(k) — need to confirm BAH match terms (offer call 2026-05-06 should have covered this; check notes).

## What's NOT in the Benefits PDF — Ask the Recruiter

These are gaps the PDF doesn't address. Get answers before signing:

1. **Annual bonus target.** PDF mentions "bonus and recognition programs" but doesn't quantify. Senior IC bonus targets at federal contractors typically 5-15% of base. Ask: "What's the target bonus percentage for this role/level, and what's the typical payout history?"
2. **Sign-on bonus.** Not mentioned. Worth asking if there's flexibility, especially if comp comes in below BAH's $145K.
3. **Salary review cadence.** Annual? Mid-year? Tied to certs?
4. **Remote work setup.** Stipend? Equipment? Internet reimbursement? (No mention in PDF — federal contractors vary widely.)
5. ~~PTO specifics~~ — **resolved 2026-05-15: unlimited in practice.** Ask Aakash at R2 about typical IronSled team usage (15-25 day norm vs performative-unlimited).
6. **Holiday floating days.** Standard 10 federal listed — any floating/personal days on top?
7. **Medical premium share specifics.** "Significant portion" is marketing language. Ask for actual employee monthly contribution numbers for each plan tier (single / +spouse / family).

## Hard-Stop Items (Don't Forget)

- **1-year service requirement on 401(k) is a real cost** if Thomas leaves within 2-3 years. Compare BAH vesting.
- **STD cap of $2,500/wk** is below his salary equivalent at $145K — relevant only if he has high-disability-risk personal context.
- **Wellness $150/yr** and **vision = voluntary** are minor — don't let them frame as "extra benefits."
- **No equity / stock mentioned anywhere in the benefits PDF.** Confirm LMI's corporate structure (historically an FFRDC / not-for-profit; verify current status) so you know whether equity, profit-sharing, or partnership-style payouts could be on the table — likely all cash + benefits + 401(k).
